About Me

Shirin Shoushtari

Hi, I'm Shirin, from Iran! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Systems Engineering at Washington University in St. Louis, advised by Prof. Ulugbek S. Kamilov. My research lies at the intersection of:

  • Computational Imaging
  • Inverse Problems
  • Generative Models
  • Out-of-Distribution Detection
  • Optimization

I develop theory and algorithms for deep learning-based imaging inverse problems, with emphasis on optimization-driven methods, generative models, and robustness under distribution shift. My work spans convergence analysis, out-of-distribution detection, and distribution shift measurement, with applications to computational and quantitative MRI. Going forward, I am particularly interested in flow matching, its deep connections to optimization and minimization theory, and how these connections can be exploited to design principled solvers for imaging inverse problems with provable guarantees.

Outside of research, I enjoy reading—especially literature, classical works, and philosophy—as well as classical music and film. I also enjoy learning languages and exploring different cultural perspectives.
